Short, practical videos that help everyday people spot scams and protect their privacy.

What we do

ScamShield publishes short educational videos on YouTube and TikTok about online scams, account security and digital privacy. Every video explains one concrete threat and what you can do about it.

About the ScamShield publisher app

ScamShield uses a small private application to publish its own videos to its own YouTube channel and TikTok account, and to read basic statistics (views, likes, watch time) of those videos so we can learn which topics help viewers most. The app is used only by the channel owner and does not access any other user's data.
