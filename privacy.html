🛡️ ScamShield

Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the ScamShield publisher application ("the App", "we") handles information. The App is a private tool operated by the owner of the ScamShield YouTube channel and TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

1. Who uses the App

The App is used exclusively by the channel owner to manage the owner's own accounts. No other users sign in to the App.

2. Information we access

  • YouTube (Google) data: via the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, the App uploads videos to the owner's channel and reads statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, average view duration, subscribers gained).
  • TikTok data: via the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API, the App reads the owner's basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID), publishes videos to the owner's TikTok account, and reads public statistics of the owner's own videos.

The App does not access data of viewers, followers or any other third party.

3. How we use information

Information is used only to publish the owner's videos and to analyse which of the owner's videos perform best, so future videos can be improved. We do not sell, rent, share or use the data for advertising.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer only. They are not transferred to any server operated by us or to third parties, other than the official Google and TikTok APIs themselves.

5. Data retention and deletion

Statistics are kept as long as they are useful for analysis. The owner can delete all locally stored data at any time and can revoke the App's access at any time via Google Account permissions and TikTok's "Manage app permissions" settings.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App uses YouTube API Services; see also the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly process children's data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always published on this page.
